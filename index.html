The face behind it

Rinchen Wongdhen.

Raised in Majnu-ka-Tilla and schooled in hospitality at Les Roches in Switzerland, Rinchen runs Wongdhen Cafe and its bakery. In 2024, after training at Italy's Gelato Naturale Academy, he opened Wongdhen Gelato next door. It's now a Times Food & Nightlife Award winner too.

“Put in a lot of sweat and time to bring this idea out.”Rinchen, on opening Wongdhen Gelato, 2024
