Our story

Raised in MKT. Cooking for it.

Wongdhen Cafe is run by Rinchen Wongdhen, who grew up in Majnu-ka-Tilla, studied hospitality in Switzerland, and now runs a café, a bakery and a gelateria in the neighbourhood.

01 · Majnu-ka-Tilla

A café in the lanes he grew up in.

Majnu-ka-Tilla, MKT to everyone in Delhi, is the Tibetan colony on the banks of the Yamuna. Wongdhen Cafe sits in its lanes, at 40 New Aruna Colony.

By 2023 people were already writing about its calm rooms, hand-stretched garlic breads and Japanese cheesecakes. Today it's a café that runs from breakfast to live music: Asian and Continental food, a bakery that bakes its own breads, and a kitchen that uses no MSG in any of its Asian dishes.

“From being raised in MKT to now shaping how MKT eats.”In Your District Delhi, June 2026

02 · Switzerland & Italy

Trained abroad, brought home.

Rinchen studied at Les Roches, the Swiss hospitality school. In April 2024 he went to Italy for a course at the Gelato Naturale Academy, and his mentors from the academy later flew to Delhi to help set up the machines and the first flavours.

That training became Wongdhen Gelato, which opened right next to the café in September 2024, built, in his words, “with the help of my parents and my team.”

03 · Wongdhen Gelato

Beer gelato, masala chai, and Tsampa.

The flavours made the papers: a beer gelato, masala chai, a masala nimbu sorbet, protein gelato, sugar-free options, and a Tsampa gelato that Time Out called “a nod to Rinchen's Tibetan roots.” In August 2026 a second Wongdhen Gelato opened in GK 2.
